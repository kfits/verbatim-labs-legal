Privacy Policy

Closeout for Jira, by Verbatim Labs · Last updated: 14 September 2026

This policy explains how Closeout for Jira ("the App"), provided by Verbatim Labs ("we", "us"), handles data when you install and use it in your Atlassian Cloud environment. We have deliberately built the App to process as little data as possible and to keep your data inside Atlassian's cloud.

In short: Closeout runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. It does not send your data to any external server or third-party service, it does not use any external AI provider, and we (Verbatim Labs) do not have access to your Jira or Confluence content.

1. Who we are

Verbatim Labs is the developer of Closeout for Jira, a Forge-native app distributed through the Atlassian Marketplace. For any privacy question, contact us at fitzpax@gmail.com.

2. What the App accesses

To generate a project or epic closure report, the App reads the following from the Atlassian Cloud site where it is installed, using Atlassian's own APIs:

  • Jira issue fields (such as summaries, dates, issue types, status history, and time-tracking values) for the project or epic you run a report on;
  • Jira issue comments for that project or epic, which are analysed to draft the report's narrative sections;
  • Confluence space information, to publish the finished report as a page;
  • Information you type into the App yourself (such as a project objective, scope, or budget figures) when you choose to provide it.

The App only accesses this data when you actively run it, and only for the project or epic you have selected.

3. Where processing happens

All processing happens within Atlassian's cloud infrastructure:

  • No external servers. The App has no backend of its own. It runs on Atlassian Forge, Atlassian's hosted serverless platform.
  • No external AI services. The narrative sections of the report are generated by Atlassian Rovo, Atlassian's own AI capability, running on Atlassian's platform. Your content is not sent to any third-party AI provider.
  • No data leaves Atlassian. The App does not transmit your data to Verbatim Labs or to any other external service.

4. What we store

While a report is being generated, the App temporarily holds the draft report sections in Forge storage — Atlassian's own hosted key-value storage within your installation. This is working data used to assemble your report; it stays within your Atlassian environment and is overwritten each time you generate a new report for the same project or epic. Verbatim Labs cannot access this storage.

The finished report is published as a Confluence page in the space you choose. From that point it is ordinary Confluence content, governed by your own Atlassian instance and its retention settings — not by the App.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store Atlassian user account API tokens.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data to train any model.
  • We do not send your data to any server or service outside Atlassian's cloud.
  • We, as the developer, cannot read your Jira or Confluence content.

6. Permissions the App requests

The App requests only the permissions it needs to do its job: to read Jira work data, to add a comment linking a published report back to a Jira issue (when you ask it to), to read Confluence spaces and create a report page, and to use Forge storage for the draft assembly described above. Each permission maps directly to a feature you control.

7. Third parties

The App relies only on the Atlassian platform (Jira, Confluence, Forge, and Rovo). Your use of those products is governed by Atlassian's Privacy Policy. We do not integrate any other third-party service.

8. Data subject requests

Because the App does not store your content outside your own Atlassian environment, requests to access or delete data are handled through your Atlassian instance. If you have a question about the App specifically, contact us at fitzpax@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be reflected here.
